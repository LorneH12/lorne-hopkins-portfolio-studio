Privacy & portfolio analytics

Updated October 9, 2026.

This professional portfolio uses optional Microsoft Clarity analytics only after you choose Allow analytics. Declining does not affect access to the portfolio, documents or training samples.

What analytics measures

With permission, Clarity collects page views, interactions, scrolling, device/browser information, approximate location and session replays. We use these to understand interest in work examples, training samples, résumé and cover-letter links, and contact actions. Microsoft processes this information under its privacy statement. See Clarity terms.

Input fields and the embedded course area are masked. We do not send names, email addresses or recruiter identifiers through custom tracking. Advertising storage is denied. Browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals disable the optional analytics.

Your choice

The Privacy choices button in the portfolio footer lets you decline or withdraw permission. Your preference is stored locally for up to six months and applies to these two original portfolios on this domain. Withdrawing reloads the page without loading Clarity; it does not delete previously collected records. Browser storage restrictions may prevent remembering your choice.

Limits and external links

Analytics covers consenting visitors, so totals are incomplete. A document click does not establish that a download completed or that a résumé was read. Direct PDF visits and offline PDF reading are not measured. We cannot reliably identify a visitor by name. Source/campaign labels indicate the link used, not proof of the visitor’s identity. Contact-link clicks do not establish that a message was sent or an interview resulted.

Training samples and external sites have their own behavior and privacy practices. Portfolio tracking measures course launches, not lesson answers or completion. The host, GitHub Pages, may process technical request data independently of this optional analytics.

Qualtrics + AI-assisted analysis

I used feedback to recommend
more demos and Q&A.

I built feedback systems using surveys, session chats and transcripts, and stakeholder reviews. The insights helped our team shift from videos and instruction to interactive demos and Q&A.

See the insight, pivot & observations

Our original model prioritized consistency. Feedback revealed disengagement and frustration. I used ChatGPT to help identify recurring questions, themes and underlying issues, then communicated findings to inform our approach.

The team pivoted to encourage curiosity and participation. After launch, we observed higher tool and resource use, greater self-enablement and reduced negative sentiment. These are qualitative program observations, not results attributable solely to one format change.
